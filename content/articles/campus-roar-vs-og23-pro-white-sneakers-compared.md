Campus owns the budget sneaker space in India, and two models keep coming up in every under-Rs-1,000 recommendation list: the Roar and the OG-23 Pro. Both are white-led colourways, both are everyday casual shoes, and the choice between them is less about quality and more about what you want them for.

## Campus Roar: The All-Rounder

The Roar in white/green is the sneaker most people picture when they think "budget white sneaker". It is a chunky, cushioned everyday shoe that works with jeans, joggers and even a casual kurti-and-sneaker outfit, which is exactly the pairing that has taken over college fashion in the last two years.

Its outsole and midsole are built for daily walking: campus commutes, metro rides, long days on your feet. The trade-off is that the design is sportier, so it reads casual rather than clean-minimal.

## Campus OG-23 Pro: The Clean Minimal Look

The OG-23 Pro in white/light grey sits on the other side of the brief. It is a cleaner, low-profile silhouette that leans into the minimalist white-sneaker look: the kind of shoe that pairs with chinos, cargo pants and straight jeans without shouting.

If your wardrobe is more western-casual than sporty, this is the one that looks more expensive than it is. The trade-off is comfort tuning that leans lifestyle rather than performance, so for all-day walking the Roar has the edge.

## The Verdict

Buy the Roar if the shoe is your daily driver and comfort matters more than the silhouette. Buy the OG-23 Pro if you already own a comfortable daily pair and want the clean white sneaker that photographs well and pairs with more outfits.

One note for both: buy white sneakers half a size up if you wear thick socks, and order from listings with recent Indian reviews on sizing, because budget sneakers vary more between batches than premium ones do.
